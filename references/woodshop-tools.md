# Essential woodshop tools (solid-wood furniture, 1-2 people)

A starting list for a small professional shop that builds solid-wood furniture with real joinery (hand-cut dovetails, mortise and tenon, laminations). Prices were read from retailer or maker pages on 2026-11-25 (**V**, verified) or are estimates (**E**) to be priced before buying. Prices are before sales tax and freight. Remove anything the owner already has, and re-check every price on the day you buy.

## Total

About **$31,000 to $46,000** for everything above. The largest swing items are the jointer-planer quote and the electrical work, so get those two priced first.

## Phase it if needed

1. Day one: bench, hand tools, bandsaw, table saw, extractor, clamps, safety (about $13,000-17,500).
2. Add the jointer-planer and cyclone when rough lumber volume justifies it.
3. Add the drill press, router table and Domino as projects need them.

## Notes

- Buying used (machines especially) can cut 30-50%; check the motor phase and voltage against the space.
- Ask the space about phase and amps before ordering any machine; many come in single-phase and three-phase versions.
- Keep receipts in the LLC's document folder; equipment is a business asset for the accountant.
